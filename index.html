Altend Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Altend helps you track purchases, recurring payments, and expiration dates. This policy describes how the current app handles the information you enter.

Information you enter

You may enter a first name for the Home greeting, item and expiry details, scheduled bills or subscriptions, payment history, purchase amounts, dates, categories, notes, currency preferences, and reminder settings. You do not need to create an account or connect a bank account.

Storage and data collection

Altend stores its records and preferences on your device using Apple’s SwiftData and local preferences. The current app does not send those records to an Altend-operated server and does not include advertising or third-party analytics services. Depending on your device settings, Apple may include app data in device backups.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, Altend schedules reminders locally through iOS. Notification content can include item names, payment amounts, and dates. Your iPhone’s notification preview settings control how much of that text is visible while the device is locked.

Importing, exporting, and sharing

You can import CSV data and create JSON backups, CSV exports, and PDF reports. These files may contain personal and financial details. Exported files are not independently encrypted or password-protected by Altend. You decide whether to save or share them through Apple’s system sharing options; any destination you choose may have its own privacy practices. Temporary generated files can remain in Altend’s temporary storage until iOS clears them or Altend performs applicable cleanup.

Deleting information

You can edit or remove records in Altend. Removing the app generally removes its local app data, subject to any device backups managed by Apple. Deleting Altend does not delete copies you previously saved to Files, iCloud Drive, email, or another destination; remove those separately if needed.

Policy changes and contact

We may update this policy if Altend’s features or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact Altend at altend.support@gmail.com.
