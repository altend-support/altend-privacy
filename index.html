Altend Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

Altend helps you track expenses, recurring payments, investment contributions, and expiration dates. This policy describes how the current iPhone app handles information that you provide or choose to import.

Information you provide

You can enter a first name for your Home greeting; expiry items and dates; purchases, payment schedules and payment history; investment contributions with an investment name or account label, amount, date, currency, and optional notes; transaction descriptions, categories, and notes; optional manually entered monthly income (including currencies and month-specific amounts); and preferences such as reminder settings. Altend does not require an account, bank login, brokerage login, or direct connection to a financial institution.

Local storage and use

Altend uses Apple’s SwiftData and local preferences to store app records and settings on your iPhone. It uses this information to display your trackers, organize spending, payments, and investment contributions, calculate cash-flow analytics and reports (including optional income comparisons), match approved statement transactions to payments, and schedule reminders. The current app does not upload those records to an Altend-operated server and does not include advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or cross-app tracking. Depending on your iPhone settings, app data may be included in backups managed by Apple.

Bank-statement PDFs and text recognition

When you select a bank-statement PDF from Files, Altend reads it on your device. It uses PDFKit for selectable text and Apple’s Vision framework for on-device text recognition (OCR) on supported scanned pages. Altend suggests transaction details and categories, but does not save imported purchases or mark scheduled payments paid until you review the proposed actions and approve the final import. OCR and transaction classification can make mistakes or miss rows; check dates, amounts, descriptions, and payment directions before approving. The selected source PDF remains in Files and is not copied into Altend’s saved records. Approved transaction details and locally derived identifiers may be stored to support tracking and help detect repeat imports. Altend does not send your bank statements to an external OCR service.

CSV imports, exports, and backups

You may import CSV files, restore JSON backups, and generate CSV exports, JSON backups, and PDF reports. These files can contain personal or financial details, including investment contribution records. They are processed or created on your device. Exported files are not independently encrypted or password-protected by Altend. You choose whether and where to save or share them through iOS; destinations such as Files, iCloud Drive, email, and other apps are subject to their own privacy practices. Temporary generated files may remain in app storage until iOS or Altend clears them. A JSON backup includes supported app records, preferences, and any optional income amounts you entered, but may not include every device preference, such as your Home greeting name.

Local notifications

If you permit notifications, Altend schedules reminders on your iPhone. Notifications may show item names, payment amounts, and dates. You can disable reminders in the app or change notification permissions and lock-screen preview visibility in iPhone Settings.

Data management and deletion

You can edit or delete saved records and optional income amounts in Altend. Removing the app generally removes its local app data from that device, but may not remove Apple-managed backups or files you separately exported or saved. If you saved a CSV, PDF, or JSON file to Files, iCloud Drive, email, or another destination, delete that copy separately. Altend has no remote user account or Altend-operated server copy of your records for us to delete.

The public policy website

The public version of this policy is hosted through GitHub Pages. Visiting that website may involve processing of technical request information by GitHub as the hosting provider under its own privacy terms. The policy page does not include Altend-added advertising or analytics scripts, and visiting it does not upload your bank statements or local app records.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as Altend’s features and data practices change. For privacy questions, contact altend.support@gmail.com.
